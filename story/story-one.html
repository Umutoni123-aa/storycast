The things we carry

A conversation about memory, belonging, and home.

Narrated by Sarah

Listen

Transcript

Narrator: When I left home, I packed light. One bag. A few clothes. But the real things I carried did not fit in a bag.

Narrator: I carried my grandmother's voice, humming while she cooked. I carried the smell of rain on red soil. I carried a name that people at home say softly, and people here often say wrong.

Narrator: At first I thought carrying so much would make me tired. But slowly I understood something. These things were not weight. They were roots.

Narrator: Home is not only a place we leave. It is something we keep, and something we build again, wherever we land.

Narrator: So, what do you carry?
