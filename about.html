About & Access

Stories for everyone.

Stories help us understand lives beyond our own. StoryCast is a space for voices, experiences, and moments worth paying attention to. We bring audio and text together so that more people can experience each story in a way that works for them.

How we make StoryCast accessible

Read
Every audio story has a full transcript on the page.
Listen
Audio stories include native controls and a visible transcript.
Navigate
All links and controls work with a keyboard and have a visible focus outline.
Understand
Clear headings, descriptive links, and readable text.
Perceive
Strong color contrast, and color is never the only clue.
